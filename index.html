Paid Media Architecture — Separate From Main Website Navigation

Meta Campaign Landing Pages

Landing Page 01 — The MCA Lifeline™

High-intent campaign. Designed to convert owners already feeling MCA payment pressure.

1. Campaign-specific hero + consultation CTA
2. “Does this sound familiar?” pain recognition
3. MCA Lifeline 3-step solution
4. Life Advisors Difference / proof
5. Tim authority + trust
6. Short qualification form / consultation

Landing Page 02 — Before You Take Another MCA…

Education-first campaign. Interrupts the reflex to stack another advance and creates a lower-pressure lead path.

1. Strong interruption headline + Tim video
2. 5 questions / educational checklist
3. Cost / stacking / cash-flow education
4. Alternatives / “understand your options”
5. Life Advisors Difference
6. Confidential consultation CTA
Strategic note: Paid Meta traffic should land on campaign-specific pages so creative, messaging, qualification and conversion can be measured independently. The main website has the broader job of establishing brand credibility, education, differentiation and trust.
